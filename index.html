This is H

Infrastructure doesn’t have to be complicated.

We help charities, iwi, hapū and community organisations make confident decisions about the places they own and use.

Whether you’re buying, leasing, maintaining or developing property, H helps you navigate infrastructure with clarity.

Independent advice. Practical support. The right expertise at the right time.

What can H help with?

Start with the problem, not a service list.

Most infrastructure questions don’t arrive labelled. They sound like this.

We need to buy a property.

H helps you think it through before you commit — what the property needs to do for you, what to check before you buy, and how it fits your organisation’s longer-term plans.

We’re planning a new facility.

From the first idea through to feasibility and a business case, H helps you scope the project properly before significant money is spent.

Our buildings keep breaking.

Recurring problems usually point to a maintenance or capital planning gap. H helps you understand the pattern and build a realistic plan to get ahead of it.

We don’t know where to start.

That’s a normal place to start. H helps you work out what the actual question is, and what needs to happen first.

We’re struggling with compliance.

H helps you understand what applies to your building, what’s genuinely urgent, and who needs to be involved to address it properly.

We’re managing too many consultants.

H can sit across the advice you’re receiving, help it join up, and make sure it’s pointed at your organisation’s actual priorities.

We need a long-term property plan.

H helps you build a realistic, sequenced plan for your property portfolio — one your board or funders can actually follow.

We need someone on our side.

H works for your organisation, not a builder, landlord or consultant. Our only interest is your outcome.

We’ve received a notice or technical report and don’t understand what it means.

H can read it with you, explain what it actually requires, and help you decide what to do next.

Our landlord says something is our responsibility and we’re not sure.

H helps you understand your lease, what it obligates you to, and how to respond with confidence.

We have a quote and want an independent view.

H can review a quote against the scope of work and tell you plainly whether it looks reasonable.

We need to decide whether to renovate, relocate or do nothing.

H helps you weigh the real options against your mission, budget and timeframe — including the option of doing nothing yet.

What makes H different

Small by design.

H is intentionally lean. Rather than building layers of management or carrying unnecessary overhead, H combines trusted relationships, modern systems and the right specialist expertise for each challenge.

Lean

Small by design.

We avoid unnecessary layers and overhead so effort stays focused on creating value.

Practical

Advice that leads to action.

We simplify complex infrastructure issues so organisations can make informed decisions.

Independent

Always on your side.

H does not benefit from recommending unnecessary design, construction or consultant services.

Technology-forward

Always evolving.

H continually evaluates technology, automation and AI-enabled tools that can improve planning, reporting, decision-making and infrastructure management.

Specialists when needed

The right expertise at the right time.

H does not pretend to hold every technical discipline internally. Legal, engineering, planning, design, compliance and other specialist expertise is brought in when genuinely required.

“H does not measure growth by headcount. It measures growth by the value it creates.”

About H

A different kind of infrastructure organisation.

H was created after seeing how often charities and community organisations were expected to navigate significant property and infrastructure decisions without dedicated in-house expertise.

The result can be avoidable risk, fragmented advice, unnecessary cost, unclear accountability, and projects becoming more complicated than they need to be.

H exists to provide a trusted point of support.
